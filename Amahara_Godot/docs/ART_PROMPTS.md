## Menu — title.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original title screen background for Amahara: Juramento de Guerra, a pixel-art top-down Japanese mythic action adventure. Create a beautiful wide 16:9 pixel art landscape at dusk: an ancient Japanese mountain village with layered tiled roofs and amber paper lanterns beside a stream, bamboo, a red torii marking the distant shrine approach, violet supernatural mist in mountains. On the right foreground a young adult black-haired warrior seen from behind in deep crimson kimono and dark hakama holding a katana, a subtle gold mark on his arm; mortal, restrained, no oversized armor. Strong deliberate pixel clusters, detailed 32-bit-era craft, no smooth painting. Left half dark forest and mist with quiet negative space for title and menu UI which will be rendered separately. Rich indigo, muted jade, vermilion, antique gold. Original designs, no copied game assets. No text, no watermark, no interface.

## Ren — ren_atlas.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Production game sprite atlas PNG with actual transparent background, exact canvas 2048x1024. EXACT GRID 8 columns and 4 rows of cells, every cell 256x256, no margins, no lines, no text. Each cell contains the SAME ORIGINAL full body young Japanese male samurai Ren with black tied hair, crimson layered kimono, dark navy hakama pants, straw sandals, dark katana scabbard and subtle gold arm mark. Precise detailed modern 32-bit PIXEL ART, each art pixel a 4x4 block, no smooth gradients no antialias, artist-made silhouette not rectangles. Top-down RPG 3/4 overhead perspective. The figure is about 100 pixels wide and 156 pixels tall (25x39 logical pixels), soles centered at (128,208) within each cell, ample empty padding so sword action fits within cell. Rows from top to bottom: facing DOWN, LEFT, RIGHT, UP. Columns left to right in EACH row: 1 idle relaxed katana sheathed, 2 walking left foot forward, 3 walking right foot forward, 4 sword windup raised drawn katana, 5 horizontal slash extended katana, 6 reverse slash with opposite arm position, 7 strong downward katana finishing strike, 8 recoiling hurt pose. Keep same character identity proportions and pixel size across every pose. Katana blade thin ivory with gold hilt. Sprite sheet must be usable in a game, strict aligned regular grid, transparent background no ground no shadows no labels no border no other objects. All 32 cells filled.

## Verificação do resultado

Saídas reais: menu 1672×941 RGB, Ren 1774×887 RGBA, props 1254×1254 RGBA. Atlas gerados não têm dimensões exatas do pedido. A leitura de metadados determinou recortes e escalas em runtime. A pose secundária de Yuna não corresponde perfeitamente ao pedido de costas e não é usada como direção traseira. A revisão visual do usuário continua necessária.

## Ações de Ren — ren_actions.png

Ferramenta imagegen integrada; novo atlas derivado da referência de identidade ren_atlas.png. Prompt final:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent pixel-art spritesheet extension for a Godot top-down action game. Reference image is Ren Kurogane identity and pixel art style reference only, preserve the same young adult black-haired man with high tied hair, crimson haori, dark navy hakama, sandals, katana and restrained gold embroidery. Create a NEW square PNG atlas with true transparent alpha, exactly four columns and four rows of equally sized cells with generous margin, 16 full-body sprites. Rows correspond strictly to facing down/front, left, right, up/back, maintaining the same elevated top-down perspective and identical human proportions as reference. Columns: 1 low forward dash crouch with katana held behind; 2 standing casting gesture with forward open palm and tiny gold palm mark no large glow; 3 falling to one knee weakened while bracing with sheathed katana; 4 final defeated body lying on side at ground level, non-graphic, still fully inside its cell, no blood. Same foot anchor and same consistent figure scale in each cell, use transparent space above lying pose. Detailed deliberate crisp pixel clusters, jade shadow highlights, rich dark red fabric, human readable silhouette, no smooth gradients, no scenery, no grid lines, no labels, no text, no checkerboard pattern, no overlapping cells, no effects outside silhouette. The final asset is usable sprite art, not a concept sheet.
